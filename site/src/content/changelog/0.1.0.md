The first packaged build of Wordy, for Apple Silicon Macs and x86_64 Linux. Everything
below is new.

## Writing

- Rich-text prose editor on a Loro document: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough,
  highlight colours, smart quotes and dashes, inline comments, find and replace, rich
  copy and paste.
- Manuscript tree of acts, chapters and scenes with drag-and-drop, duplicate, status,
  tags and placeholders.
- Scene versions with a live diff and one-click restore.
- Focus mode, typewriter scrolling, split panes, a command palette and keyboard
  navigation through the tree.
- Spellcheck with Hunspell dictionaries and a personal dictionary.

## World and notes

- World space with character, location, culture, system, object and custom sheets:
  fields, relations, aliases and attachments, edited in a Sheet tab beside the text.
- Explicit entity links plus auto-detected mentions of names and aliases, with backlinks
  and a reference pane.
- Notes space for research and outlines, searchable with the rest of the project.

## Goals, reports and export

- Daily word goal, streak, manuscript target and deadline on the dashboard.
- Reports by chapter, by day and by entity. Tasks collected from `[TODO]` markers.
- Export to `.docx`, `.epub`, `.pdf` and Markdown, quote snippets to PNG, and the whole
  project to a zip.

## Storage and sync

- A project is a folder: one Loro snapshot with full history, a readable JSON mirror,
  rolling backups and attachments by content hash.
- Optional sync through a Wordy account: link each machine with a passkey, and a project
  stays live on all of them. Changes, attachments and the personal dictionary merge
  without conflicts. Share a project with an editor or a reader from the website.
- Crash recovery and automatic history compaction after a year of edits.

## Platform

- Native Rust on GPUI, light and dark themes, macOS 12+ (Apple Silicon) and Linux
  (Wayland, X11 fallback).
- Closing the window or quitting always saves first.
- Linux per-user installer and launcher entry; macOS `Wordy.app` bundle (unsigned).
